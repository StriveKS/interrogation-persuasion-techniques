You’ve sat in this room before.

A meeting booked for 5 p.m. A compliment before the price. “We already know everything.” None of it is an accident. Four of the 14 signals inside:

The room was chosen for you

You notice: an uncomfortable chair, light in your eyes, a meeting at the end of the day.

You do: ask to change seats, open the window, or reschedule.

The compliment arrives too early

You notice: praise that is too specific, too soon, or favors before any request.

You do: thank them and move on. Never accept a favor before you know the price.

“We already know everything.”

You notice: closed folders and documents you are not allowed to touch.

You do: ask to see them. A refusal to share is information too.

Three questions before you answer one

You notice: topics that jump, urgency nobody explains.

You do: slow down. “One thing at a time.” Whoever sets the tempo controls the answer.
